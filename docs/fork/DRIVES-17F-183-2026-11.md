**Most of 17f's overrun happens above 0.8 m/s, so no stop-entry fix can recover it (M + I).**
- In the 0.5–1.0 m/s band, the model's own starting speed read **0.24–0.30 m/s below the real speed**. On last week's stops it was within −0.07 to +0.16. At 0.79 m/s the model believed 0.44 m/s and asked for only −0.26 m/s².
- Splitting the overrun from −5 s: the plan's own rest point accounts for 0.77 m. The request being softer than the model's own plan adds +1.42 m, and the 1.2–0.9 m/s coast adds +1.25 m.
- Why the model under-read its speed is not known. It was night and downhill, but 161 (night, −2.2°) did not show it.

**Grade band, a choice that is not recommended now.** A band reaching −2.62° would have latched 17f at 0.79 m/s and saved **0.64 m** (0.54–0.74), with deceleration at wheels-zero 0.51 (max 0.59). The −2.5° limit is there because the stopping state's −0.8 target has no grade term. Last week's synthetic replays from 0.8 m/s need 1.04–1.45 m at −2° to −3° with K ≥ 2.4, but 1.6–3.8 m at K 1.5. One stop on the edge is not enough to widen it. **Keep −2.5° for push19** and revisit once more downhill stops have been logged with Fix A on.

**What Fix A will and won't feel like:**
- **Will:** a shorter crawl. On the flat it also removes most of the dip: time below 90 counts went 2.77 → 0.50 s and 1.93 → 0 s.
- **Will:** the stop begins 1.5–2.5 s before rest, on the same path queue stops use.
- **Won't (or unproven):** whether the final step feels softer cannot be simulated. Last week's rough IMU regression estimated 8.2 → 9.6 m/s³ (r 0.57), which is not a reduction. The queue-stop path measures 3.3–7.0 m/s³ by the new method. So treat "feels like a queue stop" as the hypothesis the next drive tests, not a result.
- **Won't:** remove the overrun that builds above 0.8 m/s (17f), or any of the take-overs.

**The model holds the plan down (M).**

**Steady following, push18-era medians (M, skeptic-corrected):**
- **Below 35 km/h the car sits on the aggressive target** (median 0.0 to +1.4 m past it). The +2.4–3.6 m difference from the owner is the target itself (6 m + 1.25 s × v).
- The model's excess is real at 35–45 km/h (+4.5 to +5.7 m median) and 65–80 km/h (+6.1 to +6.6 m).

**Launches from a stop (M).**
- Openpilot in 1-bar rolls **2.4–2.5 s** after the lead moves; the owner rolls at 1.6 s, from a 3.1 m gap against openpilot's 5.65 m.
- He pressed the gas on 7 of 10 one-bar launches on 17f–183, at a median 2.7–2.8 s. 10 of the 12 one-bar launches are from route 182.
- Gate timeline, seconds after the lead moves:

- **Inferred:** at every stop this car shows "TAKE CONTROL / Resume Driving Manually" (`manualRestart`, because minEnableSpeed is 19 mph). The prompt comes back when the lead moves off, so some of these gas presses may be answering it.

**Close follow is not the cause (M + C).**
- Its closer gap was active in 2 % of its band on push18 (101 s) and 2.4 % on 157–183 (194 s). Its lift was active 0 %.
- What blocked it: leadHardBrake, leadBraking, hold, downhill.
- Above 17.2 km/h it hands back by design. Even fully in, it can shorten the gap by at most 0.85 m.

### 2b. Cause (with the skeptic's corrections applied)

1. **The model's acceleration is the lowest candidate** in rolling pull-aways from about 10 to 80 km/h. On the four one-bar replays it costs **7.6 / 7.9 / 10.7 / 12.1 m** with the 1.6 m/s² cap (the uncapped figures of 12.8 and 9.7 m were overstated), and about 0 where it did not set the plan.
2. **The MPC assumes a gentle lead acceleration will fade** (`aLeadTau` 1.5 while |aLeadK| < 0.5). This costs a median of about 1 m when the MPC sets the plan.
3. **Launch gates add about 0.9 s** against the owner (I: about 1.6 m of extra gap).
4. **The 1-bar target is longer than how the owner follows.** This is the target, not a fault.

### 2c. The lever tried, and why it is not recommended (S)

**The lever.** `PullAwayLift`, in close_follow.py (diff in `fix-pullaway/fix/`):
- It raises the e2e acceleration toward the plan's own MPC candidate, never past it or past cruise or Speed Limit Assist, by at most 0.6 m/s².
- It acts only at 22 km/h and above, with a radar lead faster than the car and a gap 2–6 m beyond the static target.
- It has hard trips for a braking lead, FCW, the model's brake or stop flag, TTC under 3 s, a new track, downhill, and pedals.
- Its 14 tests pass. With the lift off, the code matches push18 bit for bit on 312 of 312 closed-loop runs.

**Replay** (gap at the end of the episode, in m):

| episode | logged | strict lift | 40 km/h cap | stop-margin gate | model removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 180 15:41:54 | 36.59 | **32.84** | 36.59 | 36.59 | 26.04 |
| 15d 01:20:55 | 31.66 | **27.44** | 30.20 | 31.66 | 23.76 |
| 17f 01:26:41 | 36.47 | 36.47 (downhill trip) | 36.47 | 36.47 | 28.90 |

**Closed-loop safety.** Family F1H: a pull-away to 30–80 km/h, then the lead stops at −3, −4 or −6 m/s². 936 pairs per variant, against push18:

| variant | new contacts (push18 kept > 0.4 m) | notes |
|---|---|---|
| strict lift | **179** (design plant 114, measured 2.95 m/s² plant 65). By top speed: 30 km/h 0, 40: 1, 50: 21, 60: 64, 80: 93 | impact speed up to +27.8 km/h |
| 40 km/h cap | **6** | worst gap loss 1.85 m |
| stop-margin gate | 0 | **active 0 s** in 3,867 s of push18 one-bar driving, and 0 m on every replay |

**Why it fails.** Under openpilot this car's brake tops out at about 2.1 m/s² (cfenv `brakesum.txt`; the design plant reaches 2.14). Above about 35 km/h, the extra distance the model leaves in a pull-away is the only margin for a lead that then stops hard. Spending it turns push18's near misses into contacts. That breaks the rule that a fix must keep the hard-brake margins proven in close follow's sims.

**Keeping close follow in during a pull-away is ruled out.** The release13 and round-2 reviews showed contacts that way, and it could gain at most 0.85 m anyway.

**What follows from the sims (S, I).** With the model in, peak brake demand after a pull-away was 1.9–2.0 m/s² (15d ×2, 180). With the MPC alone it rose to 3.0–3.3. The model's slowness is currently margin in this scenario, and the MPC alone (experimental off) has less of it. So turning experimental mode off is not a safe way to get faster pull-aways.

**What would reopen it:** a higher openpilot brake ceiling, validated on the car.

### 2d. What the owner can do today, and next steps

- **Today:** pressing the gas while the model is the limit (AccelBoost) adds 0.05 m/s² per press, up to +0.2, above about 16 mph. It resets at disengage, and its logging is not in the parquet.
- **Next round, the launch delay** (about 0.9 s: the MPC stop flag, the 0.4 s debounce and the creep dead zone) is the one part of "slow behind cars" that may be fixable. It was not designed this round. It sits in close follow's launch-then-brake worst case (family A9), and it interacts with the stop-gap fix (skeptic bound C5).
- **Next drive:** no pull-away change, so there is nothing new to check.

### 3b. Cause (M + C)

**The rest gap is fixed at the stop entry.** The stop begins when speed falls below `STOPPING_SPEED` = 0.8 m/s (the fork's tune for this car; upstream uses 0.3) with the MPC asking less than +0.1. At 0.77 m/s that is true for any stopped lead within about 11 m. The car then rolls 0.57 m [0.47, 0.66] (C: `drive_helpers.py:17-19`, `longitudinal_planner.py:185,227`, `stopping_tune.py`).

So:
- **rest gap ≈ desired gap at 0.77 m/s (7.06–7.11 m) + approach offset − 0.57 m roll;**
- the rest gap correlates **0.94** with the approach offset.

**Gentle arrivals end long.** These are model-led approaches, a crawl that ends when the lead stops, a lead still rolling when the rule fires, and hops. They arrive near 7.1 m and rest at **6–7.4 m**.
- Since route 157, the rest gap rises with the mean plan over the last 3 s (ρ 0.66, p 0.005) and with the model's share of the last 5 s (ρ 0.53, p 0.04).

**Nothing closes the gap at rest.**
- At standstill the MPC asks for ≥ 0.1 m/s² only from about 7.7 m (S), and the model's `shouldStop` holds the stop anyway.
- The radar drops every track at 0.35 m/s on 61 of 61 stops, so the rest is camera-only.
- No engaged stop crept forward (0 of 61).

**Close follow plays no part.** It raised the plan by 0.000 on 15 of 15 one-bar stops and is faded out below 3.6 km/h. Personality changes the gap by at most 0.3 m, grade by ±0.1–0.3 m.

**Floor.** The MPC's standstill danger zone is 0.75 × STOP_DISTANCE 6 m = **4.5 m** (`long_mpc.py:43,172`). The owner's 3.9 m is inside it, so the aim is to bring the long stops back to the normal 4.9–5.4 m (skeptic bound C1), not to the owner's gap.

### 3c. Recommended fix: the stop-gap governor (`fix-stopgap/`, final version "v2b")

**What it does** (below V_ARM 2.5 m/s, behind a radar-tracked, camera-confirmed lead that is stopped or creeping at ≤ 0.5 m/s):
1. **Landing lift.** After the candidates' min() and the re-entry ramp, the plan may be raised by at most **0.30 m/s²** (rate 0.6 m/s³) toward the constant deceleration that brings the car to 0.77 m/s at 5.77 m. Today's stop then ends at about **REST_GAP 5.2 m**.
   - The stop itself is unchanged: the same 0.8 m/s entry, stopping state, soft stop and roll.
   - Brisk approaches already brake less than that profile, so they get nothing.
2. **Catch.** Only after it has lifted, it may also lower the plan by the same 0.3 so the stop cannot end inside **4.7 m**.
3. **Hazard hold, 3 s,** after any plan under −2.0, a lead braking harder than −2.5, FCW or an MPC crash count.
4. **Trips, which end it for that stop:**
   - no radar lead, a held lead, or a track change that does not continue the old range within 0.75 m;
   - a range jump over 1.5 m, or the camera missing or reading more than 1.0 m closer than the radar;
   - the queue moving off (close follow's rule: aLeadK > 0.3 or vLead > 0.5 for 0.5 s);
   - downhill steeper than −2.5°, gas, a reset, or a plan under −1.0 after a lift.
5. **Never touched:** the model's stop flag and the stop rule.

**Why it must override the MPC.** At the long stops' entry the MPC itself asked −0.15 to −0.48 m/s², within 0.1 of the model (15d −0.29 vs −0.37; 182 15:43:07 −0.48 vs −0.44; 15:46:55 −0.34 vs −0.33). A version capped at the MPC would close nothing (M).

**Replays (S).** Neither replay reproduces each logged stop exactly (about 1 m error per stop), so the evidence is the difference between the two arms of each replay.

| 180 Sun 8 Nov 15:44:12 | 6.02 → **4.88** | 6.66 → 6.21 |

- **Normal stops:** replay A, 0 of 39 got shorter. Replay B, 5 of 31 got 0.18–0.53 m shorter, none below 4.95 m.
- **Never closer than 4.88 m.**

**Safety (S).** The close-follow verifier's sim, pointed at push18 (real planner, radard, LongControl and opendbc CarController; design brake plant; model stand-in). 1,140 paired runs across seven families, including launch-then-brake, crawl-launch-brake, stop-and-go and cut-ins.

**Versions rejected:**
- **v1, defer the stop to 0.3 m/s.** It closed the creeping-lead stops, but when the queue moved off mid-hold the car never stopped. 48 of 1,320 pairs went more than 0.85 m inside the target (worst 2.37 m), and the closest point fell by up to 1.22 m.
- **v3, hold only behind a fully stopped lead.** Gained nothing on the logs, and cut-ins lost up to 0.75 m.
- **Changing STOP_DISTANCE or creeping at rest.** Not built: it means regenerating the solver, and at rest it would run on the camera alone.

**What the owner will feel:**
- **On a gentle arrival behind a stopped car:** in the last 9 km/h the braking eases by up to 0.3 m/s². The hardest plan in the last 3 s goes from −0.59…−1.15 to −0.37…−0.69. The car rolls about 1–1.5 m further at walking pace, then gives the same soft stop (about 0.5 m/s²).
- The stop comes 0.1–0.3 s later at the median, 1.75 s at most. It rests at about 4.9–5.3 m on the radar (4.5–4.9 m on the camera).
- Occasionally, after a lift, the last metre brakes up to 0.3 m/s² firmer (the catch).
- **Unchanged:** brisk arrivals, normal stops, stops after a hard brake, and creeping queues.

**Not fixed:**
- **Stops behind a still-creeping lead:** 178 Thu 5 Nov 15:28:05 (7.41 m) and 182 15:46:55 (6.06 m, also −4.3°).
- **Stops that follow a hard brake** (182 15:45:09, hazard hold).
- **Hops are probably mostly untouched.** They are inside V_ARM, but the radar is back for only 57 % of a hop, and hops were not replayed.

**Risks:**
- This is new authority: it may raise the plan above every candidate, including the MPC, by ≤ 0.3 m/s² below 9 km/h. The hazard hold, the trips and the catch bound it, and the sims bound the effect.
- Its landing assumes a 0.77 m/s entry and a 0.57 m roll. Fix A's soft-stop firm-up moved queue rests a median 0.000 m (at most 0.22 m shorter) in replay, so with both fixes a lifted stop could land up to about 0.2 m further back. That is harmless.
- The camera and radar disagree by up to 1.4 m near 6 m (the CAM_TOL 1.0 trip covers the dangerous direction).
- Elesys renumbers tracks; `swap_ok` accepts a new id that continues the old range within 0.75 m. The cut-in family came out unchanged.

  - The bumper-to-bumper distance is unknown; neither sensor is calibrated to the bumper. Measure once with a tape, stopped behind a parked car.
